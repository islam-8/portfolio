About the Award

NRSC 2026 Poster Award

The National Radio Science Conference (NRSC) is the premier scientific forum in Egypt for radio science, telecommunications, and defense electronic countermeasures. Winning 5th Place for Best Poster recognizes outstanding engineering rigor, scientific feasibility, and cutting-edge hardware design in defense and radar-tracking systems.

⚙️

A+ Capstone Project

Awarded for developing an Advanced System for Drone Detection, Tracking, and Neutralization. Built to explore the limits of resource-constrained hardware, the system integrates a quantized INT8 YOLOv8-nano model on NCNN, optimized with NEON SIMD, and managed under a real-time Linux kernel (SCHED_FIFO) to secure sub-2ms predictable latency.
